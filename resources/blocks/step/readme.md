## Description

One step of a Steps block. It starts with a heading and a line of text, and can hold any other blocks under them.

## Controls

### icon

An icon of its own, when the steps are marked with icons.

### label

A label beside the step, such as a date, when the steps run down with labels.

## Tips

- Make a step the current one from its toolbar or its panel; the steps around it follow.
