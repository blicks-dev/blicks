[Blicks Kits](/guides/travel) adds eleven sections to the block inserter, under **Patterns › Travel**. Stack them to
make a home page, an about page or a contact page in minutes, then change them like any blocks:
every part is a Blicks block you can restyle, move or remove.

The live sections fill themselves from your trips, through the same sources as [Kits templates](/guides/travel/templates): your
destinations, activities and trip types, the trips on sale, your reviews and your totals. Add a
trip and the sections follow. None of them is a WP Travel Engine block, so a page made of them loads
no WP Travel Engine file and no jQuery (see [Speed](#speed) below).

Patterns work on every theme, block or classic. On a classic theme they are the way to bring Kits
into a page.

## Insert a section

In the editor, open the inserter, choose **Patterns**, then **Travel**. Or type `/` in the page and
search: "destinations", "reviews", "enquiry". A section is inserted as its blocks, so once it is on
the page it is yours: nothing changes it when Kits updates.

## The sections

| Section | What it shows | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Home hero** | A full-width photo, a heading, a line and **Trip search**. | Trip search |
| **Featured destinations** | Your top-level destinations with most trips, as photo tiles, and a link to all trips. | Places: destinations |
| **Popular activities** | Activities with most trips, as chips ("Trekking · 12 trips"). | Places: activities |
| **Trip-type tiles** | Your trip types as photo tiles, three across. | Places: trip types |
| **Deals** | Trips on sale, with the saving, and a link to all of them. | Trips: on sale |
| **Why travel with us** | Four reasons, each with an icon, in your own words. | Your words |
| **Reviews wall** | Your average rating and number of reviews, then your best reviews of four stars and up. | Places: totals, and Reviews |
| **About the agency** | A photo, your story, and your numbers: trips, destinations and years. | Places: totals |
| **Newsletter** | A heading beside a signup form: email, consent, Subscribe. | A Blicks Form |
| **Call to action** | A dark band with "Find your trip" and "Talk to us". | Your words |
| **Contact and enquiry** | Your details beside an enquiry form that asks which trip. | A Blicks Form |

Each live section is a Blicks **Loop** with its **Comes from** set. Select the Loop to change what it
shows: pick your own destinations under **These places**, show more chips, or sort reviews by
newest.

### Sections with nothing to show

Featured destinations, Popular activities, Trip-type tiles, Deals and the Reviews wall leave the
page when they have nothing to show: no destination with trips, nothing on sale, no reviews yet. A
heading over an empty space is worse than no section. They come back by themselves once there is
something to show.

In the editor they stay, so you can still edit them, with a note in place of the cards saying that
visitors don't see the section for now. The Reviews wall goes by its reviews alone: your totals
always have something to show, so they don't keep it on the page.

## Replace the stand-ins

A few things in the sections are yours to fill in:

- **Pictures.** The hero and About the agency use small placeholder pictures that ship with Kits.
  Replace them with your photos. Place tiles show each place's own picture, or its first trip's.
- **Links.** "Our story" and "Talk to us" link to `#`. Point them at your about and contact pages.
  "All trips", "See all deals" and "Find your trip" already open your trip archive.
- **Contact details.** Contact and enquiry shows `hello@example.com`, a phone number and an address.
  Put yours in.
- **Your words.** Why travel with us, About the agency and the years in business ("15") are typed,
  not counted. Make them true for you.

The fixed words of every section (headings, labels, buttons) follow the site's language, so a German
site gets them in German where a translation exists.

## Forms

Newsletter and Contact and enquiry are Blicks Forms. What visitors send arrives in **Blicks ›
Entries** and by email, like any Blicks Form, with Blicks' spam checks and its consent box. Kits
doesn't pass newsletter signups on to a mailing service; a developer can, from Blicks'
`blicks_form_sent` hook.

### The Trip field

Contact and enquiry asks which trip with **Trip**, a field type Kits adds to Blicks Forms. You can
add it to any form: in a Form, add a Field and choose **Trip** under Travel.

- Visitors pick from your trips by name. The first choice is "Not sure yet", or "Choose a trip" when
  the field is required.
- On a trip's own page it starts on that trip, so an enquiry sent from a trip page names it without
  the visitor choosing.
- In Blicks › Entries, the email and the CSV export, an entry shows the trip's name. A trip deleted
  since shows as "Trip 123".

In the editor the Trip field draws as an empty box, since the trips are listed on the page itself;
its settings show it as Trip.

## Themes

The sections are tested on a page of all eleven, on Twenty Twenty-Five and Ollie (block themes) and
Twenty Twenty-One (a classic theme), on a wide screen and a phone: nothing wider than the screen, no
errors, the theme's header and footer around them, and empty sections left off.

## Speed

A page of all eleven sections, measured on a phone (412 px wide, fast 4G, CPU slowed four times, cold
cache) on Twenty Twenty-Five:

| | With WP Travel Engine | With test trips, all eleven drawn |
|---|---|---|
| Requests | 16 | 21 |
| Transferred | 153 KB | 165 KB |
| WP Travel Engine files, jQuery | none, no | none, no |
| Largest paint (the hero's heading) | 1.2–1.4 s | 1.1–1.2 s |

On the test site's WP Travel Engine five sections had nothing to show and left the page, which is why
it transfers less. Most of its extra time is the server's: WP Travel Engine works out a trip's
departure dates the first time a page reads the trip, here three years of daily departures.
