Kits blocks draw your trip pages, but a booking still goes through two screens that belong to WP
Travel Engine: its **booking window** (dates, packages, travellers) and its **checkout page**. Kits can
draw those two screens in your site's style: your primary colour, text colours, fonts and corners,
read from your Blicks design system. WP Travel Engine's steps, words and checkout stay exactly as they
are; only how they look changes.

## Choose the look

Go to **Blicks › Kits › Booking look** and pick one:

| Look | What visitors see |
|---|---|
| **Match my site** (recommended) | The window and the checkout in your Blicks colours, fonts and corners. Continue and Confirm Booking look like your Blicks buttons. |
| **WP Travel Engine's look** | Both screens exactly as WP Travel Engine draws them: its colour, its buttons and fonts. |

Each choice shows a small drawing of the window and the checkout in that look. Press **Save
changes**. If you use a page cache, clear it afterwards.

Until you save a choice, Kits starts on **Match my site**, unless you have set WP Travel Engine's own
primary colour in its Appearance settings. Then it starts on WP Travel Engine's look, since you chose a
colour there, and the tab says so.

## What changes with Match my site

**The booking window:**

- The window, its steps and the summary column in your surface, text and muted colours, instead of
  white with a blue-tinted column.
- The calendar as a bordered box, the picked day and start time in your primary colour.
- Traveller rows as bordered rows, with matching counters.
- Back in your primary colour; Continue drawn like a Blicks button, without the glow, in your font.
- WP Travel Engine's icons (the close cross, Back's arrow, the counters) follow the text around them.
- A visible focus outline in your focus colour for keyboard visitors.

**The checkout:**

- Its steps in your primary colour instead of WP Travel Engine's green.
- Its boxes as bordered panels in your corners.
- Fields in your font and border colour, with WP Travel Engine's floating labels kept and their
  contrast raised to 4.5:1.
- Payment methods, coupons, the trip details and the booking summary in your colours.
- Confirm Booking drawn like a Blicks button.

A dark design system carries through: on a site with a dark palette, the window and the checkout are
dark too.

## The checkout on block themes

WP Travel Engine's checkout stylesheet removes the side padding of block themes such as Twenty
Twenty-Five, which cuts the checkout page off on the left on a wide screen and on a phone. Kits puts
the padding back on the checkout page, **whichever look you choose**.

A developer can turn that fix off with the `blicks_kits_host_fix` filter (its arguments: whether to
apply it, and the WP Travel Engine stylesheet it follows, `trip-checkout`).

## Your own CSS

Kits' rules are WP Travel Engine's own selectors with one class more (two on buttons, to outrank the
button styles of classic themes such as Twenty Twenty-One). A rule of yours for these screens with a
little more specificity still wins. Kits uses `!important` only where WP Travel Engine does.

## Speed

The look is one small stylesheet (about 4 KB compressed). It loads only:

- with WP Travel Engine's booking window, when a visitor starts to book (the window's own files are
  about 100 KB compressed), and
- on WP Travel Engine's checkout page.

Trip pages, archives and the rest of your site never load it. See [Speed](/guides/travel/speed) for how Kits keeps
WP Travel Engine's own files off your pages.

## WP Travel Engine versions

Tested with WP Travel Engine 6.8 and 6.9. 6.9 redrew its checkout (labels above the fields, a new trip
details box and order summary) on colour and size settings of its own; Kits points those at your
Blicks design system, so both checkouts follow your site. On a WP Travel Engine version Kits hasn't
been tested with, Book this trip takes visitors to the trip's own page instead of opening the booking
window.

## Themes

The booking look is tested with Twenty Twenty-Five and Ollie (block themes) and Twenty Twenty-One (a
classic theme), on a wide screen and a phone: the window and the checkout in your Blicks colours and
the theme's font, nothing wider than the screen, and a booking that reaches the checkout.

On Twenty Twenty-One the checkout sits in the theme's narrow content column, so WP Travel Engine's
trip details and summary table are cramped there, whichever look you choose. That layout is WP Travel
Engine's own; the booking look changes colours, type and corners, not layout.

## Developers

- The look is the option `blicks_kits_booking_look` (`site` or `host`), and the filter
  `blicks_kits_booking_look` decides it per request.
- `blicks-kits/v1/booking-look` (administrators) reads the tab's data and saves the choice.
- An adapter that ships with Kits brings its own stylesheet for its host's screens through its
  `assets()` manifest's `style` entry; WP Travel Engine's is
  `resources/travel/hosts/wp-travel-engine/style.css`. See
  [Write an adapter](/developers/write-an-adapter#the-booking-look).
