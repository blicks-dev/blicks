How a block reacts to the pointer and the keyboard is part of its look, so it lives in the
**Style** tab, under **States**. You pick what should happen — a lift, a glow, a press — rather than
switching the whole inspector into a hover mode and restyling the block there.

<Frame caption="A card's Style tab, with States at the bottom: On hover, Blocks inside react to this, and When clicked.">
	<img src="/images/inspector/box-card-2-style.png" alt="The Style tab for a Box card, showing Text, Fill, Border and corners, Shadow and depth, Space and States" style={{ maxWidth: '280px' }} />
</Frame>

## On hover

Pick a ready-made look: **Nothing**, **Lift**, **Glow**, **Darken**, **Grow** or **Tilt**.

Above the looks, choose what the block reacts to:

- **Pointed at** — the pointer is over the block itself.
- **Its card is** — the pointer is anywhere over the card the block sits in. Use it to brighten an
  icon, or nudge an arrow, when the visitor points at the whole card. This also offers looks that
  only make sense for something inside a card: **Appear** and **Slide in**.

On a card, turn on **Blocks inside react to this** to let the blocks inside it use **Its card is**.

## When clicked

**Nothing**, **Press in**, **Push down** or **Sink** — what the block does while it is pressed. A
block that answers to its card plays it while anything on the card is pressed.

## Keyboard focus

Shown when someone reaches the block with <kbd>Tab</kbd>: **Browser** (the browser's own ring, the
default), **Ring**, **Gap**, **Glow** or **Outline**.

<Tip>
If a block reacts to the mouse, make sure it reacts to the keyboard too. Click into the page and
press <kbd>Tab</kbd> through it: every link and button should visibly change as it takes focus.
</Tip>

## Making your own

Each look has **Customize ›**, which opens it in place of the panel. Choose **Ready-made** to keep a
preset, or **Make your own** to set exactly what changes — the fill, the text color, the border,
the shadow, the size, and how far it moves. Only what you set changes; everything else stays as the
block's normal look.

To go further, **All settings** at the bottom of the Style tab lists every value the block stores,
including those for hover, focus and pressed. **Add a setting** can put any setting the block
allows on a state, a screen size or a part.

## Motion

The **Motion** tab holds the rest of what moves:

<Frame caption="A Button's Motion tab: Entrance, then Hover and click.">
	<img src="/images/inspector/button-3-motion.png" alt="The Motion tab for a Button, with Entrance effects and the Speed and Feel of hover and click" style={{ maxWidth: '280px' }} />
</Frame>

- **Entrance** — **None**, **Fade in**, **Rise** or **Zoom**. It plays once, as the block scrolls
  into view.
- **Hover and click** — how the looks from **Style ▸ States** play: **Speed** (**Instant**,
  **Quick**, **Smooth**, **Slow**) and **Feel** (**Gentle**, **Springy**, **Steady**). Set it once
  here instead of on each state.
- **Reduced motion** — for a block that keeps moving, what it does for a visitor who has asked
  their device to reduce motion: stay **Still**, **Hide**, or rest at a **Custom** size and fade.

### Reduced motion

Animations — entrances and anything built from keyframes — run only when the visitor has not asked
for reduced motion. They are written inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, so a
visitor with that setting on sees the page without them, and you do not have to handle it.

<Warning>
Reduced motion covers animations, not transitions. A hover look still changes for those visitors,
so keep hover movement small and quick.
</Warning>

## In Blicks Studio

In the Studio, the Motion tab also has **Interactions**: a block can turn on and off when clicked —
a mode, like Night or Yearly — with its **On** look styled like a hover look, and can follow or lean
toward the pointer.
